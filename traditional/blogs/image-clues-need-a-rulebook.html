Image clues need a rulebook

What Crossword Go taught me about clarity, delight, and difficulty spikes.

On Crossword Go I was the editorial checkpoint for image clues—a format where text rules don’t automatically transfer. A picture can be read many ways, and one ambiguous visual can spike difficulty across an entire puzzle.

We built guidelines from scratch: visuals had to telegraph the answer without giving it away, stay consistent with in-grid clue length limits, and still feel playful next to traditional text clues.

The hardest part is the line between “clever” and “clever if you already know what we mean.” Themed packs amplify that tension because players expect a coherent difficulty curve across dozens of levels.
