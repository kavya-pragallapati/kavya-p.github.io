Pockets: A Tragic Love Story

July 2021

Have you ever complimented a girl on her dress? If her dress has pockets, chances are, you just made her day. She’ll put her hands in her pockets with an excited grin, “it has pockets,” she’ll declare the obvious.

I was browsing for clothes the other day and realized pockets were not one of the criteria in filters. I was disappointed. Nothing new, though, I always am. It’s a never-ending saga. I look for clothes with functional pockets. I leave unsatisfied.

It has always been a point of bitterness for me. How hard can it be to add pockets to my jeans, pockets big enough to hold my phone? I could blame the phone manufacturers for making the phones bigger and bigger with every release. Unfortunately, the blame is on the clothes.

I realize this every time I hand my phone to my brother to hold onto. He has enough pockets to hold two phones, and mine is barely enough to fit one small pebble. This happened again when my friend put both mine and his phones in the same pocket. Can you believe it? One pocket was big enough to hold two phones!

This wasn’t enough to make me jealous. It’s fine, clothes are made differently, that I can understand. What’s unfair is the entire contents of my small bag fit into one pocket of men’s jeans. It was one of those days when I didn’t have a bag on me. I took the risk of carrying my phone in my pocket. Half of it was peeking out of my pocket, ready to jump if the opportunity presented itself. It almost felt like my phone wanted to run away from me. I refused to give it the opportunity. When I had to bend to tie my laces, I knew my pocket was waiting for revenge. The phone would have tumbled down, hitting the floor. A tragedy I wanted to avoid.

“Hold this for a second?” I handed my phone to my friend. He plucked it out of my hands without a second thought. I watched in awe as he placed my phone into a pocket that already had his phone and his sanitizer bottle. “The other one has keys. I don’t want to scratch your screen,” he answered in way of explanation. Little did he know my shock had nothing to do with scratches on my screen. Must be a happy life if your pockets are big enough to hold the universe.

I dream of the day when my pockets would hold the contents of my purse that way. I would be free from all bindings of purse handles. I would no longer have to adjust the strap when it digs into my shoulder. I yearn for a day when I wouldn’t have to dig through my purse to find my phone. I’ll reach into my pocket, knowing for sure that I’ll find my phone. I won’t have to sift through the wallet, the sanitizer, the earphones that have all run out of their designated places in my bag. It feels, sometimes, like the contents of my purse hate me. Ensuring I find everything except the one thing I seek.

Pockets and I will one day be star-crossed lovers no more. We’ll exist together. We won’t have to cross universes to find one another.
