Themed words and difficulty

When shared clue phrasing helps—and when it throws the whole grid off balance.

In a themed crossword, certain entries belong to the theme and the clues usually signal that relationship. If the theme is greetings around the world, you might see parallel clues like Greeting in Mumbai or Greeting in Madrid sitting beside straight definitions elsewhere in the grid.

That common phrasing is useful: it tells solvers which words are “in on the joke.” But it also changes difficulty. Depending on the theme, themed clues can become trivially easy or unfairly hard compared with the rest of the puzzle.

When I edit themed grids, I watch for that skew early. The goal is for theme entries to feel discoverable without making the surrounding fill feel like filler—or like a trap.
