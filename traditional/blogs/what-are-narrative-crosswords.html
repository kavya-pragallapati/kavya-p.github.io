What are narrative crosswords?

From themed entries to puzzles that carry a story across clues.

How do you have a narrative in a crossword puzzle? That's a great question and I am very glad you asked!

If you've ever played an NYT puzzle, you know that certain words in a crossword grid are themed words. These words can be anything, as long as they fit into the theme.

For example, if your theme is “How to say Hello in different languages,” your words might be “Hello,” “Namaste,” “Hola,” “Bonjour,” and so on. Your clues could be Greeting in New York, Greeting in Mumbai, Greeting in Madrid, Greeting in Paris.

Themed puzzles

Here's one of my themed puzzles: 2023-11-10 tautological places.

This puzzle is about place names that are tautological—a straightforward theme.

Connecting theme words

The common phrasing in all the clues establishes that they are themed words and associated clues. However, these clues can skew the difficulty of your overall puzzle, because depending on the theme they are going to be either too difficult or too easy.

Narrative puzzles
