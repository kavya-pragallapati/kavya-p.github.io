Crossword Go

Scrabble-style crosswords with in-grid clue cells—and a new editorial playbook for image clues.

Crossword Go is a scrabble-style crossword puzzle game that has in-grid clue cells. That format required new guidelines that account for shortened clue length and a careful balance between image and text clues.

It also required setting up and building new clue styles for smaller words, and reimagining crossword guidelines to fit the limited clue space.
