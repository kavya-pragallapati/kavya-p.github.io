My Work

I'm a cruciverbalist and level designer, focused on word games, puzzle mechanics, and building scalable processes.

I built a strong foundation in crossword grid construction and created themed puzzles for Daily Themed Crosswords, Crossword Explorer, and Crossword Go. I architected difficulty and progression curves for Crossword Explorer and Offline Puzzle Master, optimizing engagement and retention across multiple game modes. I also served as editor for the packs feature in Daily Themed Crossword—building creation guidelines, setting editorial standards, and maintaining feature health.

My core skills are level and difficulty design (tuning difficulty levers and balancing progression), crossword creation and editorial guidelines, and process setup and pipeline optimization.

Outside of day-to-day production, I spend free time analyzing narrative and puzzle games, always asking “How did they make this?” I look for ways to optimize or automate without sacrificing quality—balancing speed with excellence.

Crosswords are my bread and butter, but my role grew beyond grid-building—for example on Crossword Go, where I was the key editorial checkpoint for image clues. Text clues follow familiar rules; image clues needed a new playbook from scratch, with visuals clear enough that difficulty stayed steady and creative enough to keep players delighted. Balancing that line across complex themed packs is a big part of how I think about level design. On Offline Puzzle Master I also built word search grids and a progressively difficult Wordle curve, which meant teaching myself the basics of information theory along the way.
