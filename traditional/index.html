Hi, I'm Kavya Pragallapati

Welcome to my personal corner of the internet.

Here you'll find my work in word games and puzzle design, things I've written, and side projects I've picked up along the way.

I'm a crossword content creator and level designer, with experience in word games, level difficulty, and building scalable processes.

I built a strong foundation in crossword grid construction while creating themed puzzles for Daily Themed Crosswords, Crossword Explorer, and Crossword Go. I architectured difficulty and progression curves for Crossword Explorer and Offline Puzzle Master, optimizing engagement and retention across multiple game modes. I also served as editor for the packs feature in Daily Themed Crossword—building creation guidelines, setting editorial standards, and maintaining feature health.

My core skills are level and difficulty design (tuning difficulty levers and balancing progression), crossword creation and setting up editorial guidelines, and process setup and pipeline optimization. I look for ways to optimize or automate without sacrificing quality.

Crosswords are my bread and butter, but my role grew beyond grid-building—for example on Crossword Go, where I was the key editorial checkpoint for image clues. While the text clue rules were similar to normal crosswords; image clues needed a new playbook from scratch, with visuals clear enough that difficulty stayed steady and creative enough to keep players delighted. On Offline Puzzle Master I also built word search grids and a progressively difficult Wordle curve, which meant teaching myself the basics of information theory along the way.

Outside of day-to-day work, I spend free time playing and analyzing narrative / puzzle games, always asking “How did they make this?” I also thoroughly enjoy playing Overcooked, one of the few games I have a 100% completion in. Some would say I have an Overcooked obsession even, but I think all the hours that have gone into time management games like Overcooked has instilled in me the ability to see issues in proccesses and find ways to optimize them.
